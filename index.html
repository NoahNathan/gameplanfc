Common questions

What’s free and what’s Premium?

For every team size, the first 2 formations and the first 2 plays are free. Premium unlocks every formation, every attacking, defending and set-piece play, and the offside lessons.

Do I need an account?

No. Premium is an App Store subscription tied to your Apple ID, so there’s nothing to sign up for.

I got a new phone. How do I get Premium back?

Open the subscribe screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Game Plan FC. You keep Premium until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I have a beta-tester code. Where do I put it?

In the app, go to Learn and tap Redeem a code. The code gives you Premium free for the time it says. After that the subscription renews at the normal price unless you cancel, and Apple reminds you before it does.

How do I join the beta?

Email us with how many a side your team plays, or tap Join the beta under Learn in the app.
